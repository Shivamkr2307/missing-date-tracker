# Stage 1: Solution - CampusConnect Smart Deadline & Academic Calendar Hub

## Problem Statement 04: Missing Important Dates
> "Students miss important deadlines, exams and assignment dates because everything is scattered across chats and notebooks."

### Problem Analysis
In modern college and university environments, academic information is fragmented across multiple unorganized communication channels:
1. **Unstructured WhatsApp / Telegram Groups**: Class representatives and professors drop casual reminders ("Assignment 2 due Friday midnight", "Mid-term exam on 24th in Hall 3B").
2. **Scattershot LMS Portals**: Moodle, Canvas, and Google Classroom instances with non-standardized notifications.
3. **Physical Notebooks & Whiteboards**: Quick handwritten notes that get lost between lectures.
4. **Administrative Deadlines**: Fee clearance dates, exam registration windows, and lab report submissions buried in email attachments.

This fragmentation leads to high student anxiety, missed grade weightages, last-minute cramming, and administrative penalties.

---

## Proposed Solution: CampusConnect
**CampusConnect** is a unified, intelligent **Academic Deadline & Calendar Hub** designed specifically to solve date scattering for students.

### Key Capabilities
1. **Smart Chat Un-scrambler**: An AI/Regex parser allowing students to paste raw chat messages or notice board notes. The system auto-extracts dates, course codes (e.g., `CS101`, `MATH202`), categories, and titles into 1-click actionable deadlines.
2. **Urgency Visualizer & Countdown Clock**: A live ticking urgency clock banner highlighting the single most critical upcoming deadline (with red pulse alerts for overdue items).
3. **Multi-View Workspace**:
   - **Timeline List View**: Categorized into Overdue, Due Today, Next 7 Days, Later, and Completed.
   - **Interactive Calendar Grid**: Monthly visual calendar with color-coded course indicators.
   - **Subject / Course Hub**: Progress bars tracking completion percentage per course.
   - **Kanban Board**: Drag-and-drop workflow (To Do, In Progress, Submitted/Completed).
4. **Student Focus Tools**: Integrated 25-minute Pomodoro focus study timer for specific deadlines with confetti celebrations upon completion.
5. **Universal iCalendar (.ics) Export**: 1-click export to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, and Microsoft Outlook.
